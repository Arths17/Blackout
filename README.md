# BLACKOUT

You are in a dark maintenance room with a creature. Three breakers send electrical surges around the room. Lamps show where the surge is going and briefly reveal the creature.

Each generator's path is always shown on the floor. When the creature crosses one, press that generator's number to send a surge down it. The surge ricochets off the walls and hurts you too if you are in the way. Land enough shocks to restore power, then reach the open exit. A generator that hits stays offline until a different one hits. Missing makes the boss faster, the next windup shorter and the generator recovery longer. You have five health pips.

## Controls

There are five boss fights in a row, and the boss gets faster, bigger and tougher each time. Its health shows as a bar over its head. The boss starts in the top-right corner guarding the exit and comes for you the moment the fight begins, and every time you fire a generator the boss stops, winds up and lunges at it. The wind-up is the same length every time in a room and gets shorter in each new room. From the second fight on it starts attacking by itself: timed lunges, then bouncing seeds in a fan, and it speeds up when badly hurt. After each boss you open a chest: the faster you killed it, the more coins you get, and it holds a piece of the story of the Thornwood Clan, trees the Rot infected, that you have to kill to get home. After the fifth boss a results screen shows your total time and coins.

- WASD or arrow keys to move up, down, right, and left
- 1, 2 or 3: fire that generator from where it stands. You can be anywhere in the room.
- E or Space: fire the generator you are standing next to.
- 4 or F: parry the boss's touch or a seed (a short window, then a cooldown). A parry shoves you and the boss apart. Two parries in a row turn you into a super ball: bigger and yellow, it flies further, and it does double the normal damage if it hits the boss.
- Shift: a small dash.
- R: restart the boss fight
- Esc: pause and bring back this controls screen. The arrow keys there change volume, screen shake and flashes.
- Enter on the exit screen, and on the chest screens: open the chest and move on to the next fight
