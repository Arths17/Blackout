## Description (copy everything below this line into the description box)

We made this for a jam where the theme was **Bounce Back**, and we took it pretty literally.

You're stuck in a dark maintenance room with something that wants you dead. There are three breakers on the wall. Each one throws a surge of electricity along a path that's painted on the floor, and the surge ricochets off the walls and comes back. If you're standing in the way when it returns, it hurts you just as much as it hurts the monster.

So the whole game is baiting. You can't outrun it for long, but you can walk it across a wire, hit the right number key, and get out of the way before your own lightning finds you.

### How it plays

- Move with **WASD** or the arrow keys.
- Press **1, 2 or 3** to fire that breaker. You don't have to be near it, which is the point. You're the bait, not the electrician.
- Press **Shift** to parry when the boss touches you. Land two parries in a row and you send it flying with a bigger bounce that hits several times.
- **R** restarts the fight instantly. You'll use it.

Every breaker's path is always visible, so there's nothing to memorize. The skill is in where you stand and when you fire. Miss and the next windup gets shorter while the breaker takes longer to recover, so a wasted shot costs you.

### What you're fighting

Five bosses back to back. Each one is faster, bigger and meaner than the last. Past the first fight they stop waiting for you and start lunging and spraying bouncing seeds on their own, and they get angry when they're low on health.

After each kill there's a chest. The quicker you won, the more coins are inside, and there's a scrap of story in there too: who these things used to be, and why you're fighting them. The last screen adds up your total time and coins, so there's a reason to go again.

### Good to know

- It's short. A run is a handful of minutes if you're good, a lot more if you're not.
- Click the game once so it grabs your keyboard. Sound turns on with your first key press.
- Works best in a desktop browser. No touch controls.
- Headphones help. The room is dark and a lot of the tension is in the sound.
