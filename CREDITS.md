# Credits

## Art

The White Hero sprites were supplied by the user's friends in `White_Hero_Complete_LibreSprite_Animations.zip` and kept in `public/hero/`. The user confirmed their authorship. Directional idle/walk, interaction, hurt, fall and victory frames are used. Dash and punch sprites remain reference material and add no new mechanics. No third-party license is inferred; this is user-supplied team art.

`tools/import_hero.py` records source hashes and converts the used hero frames from 32×48 to 16×24 with nearest-neighbor sampling, binary transparency and the cold neutral palette. `assets/src/hero.json` contains the resulting pixel grids. The source PNGs are unchanged.

The room, props, creature, lighting effects and stencil font are original project art. `tools/make_art.mjs` exports indexed PNGs from the 30-color master palette in `src/config.ts`; `tools/palette.json` is generated from it. The code and art pipeline were developed with AI coding assistance. No image-generation tool was used for this slice.

Ashgrove was consulted read-only for its palette discipline, authored sprite grids and stepped lighting. No Ashgrove art or code was copied. Sid's prototype supplied the coherent electrical-wave motion adapted to the existing routes; its mechanics were not used.

The root-monster sheet (`public/tree-root-monster.png`) is the creature in the game. It was supplied by the team; its provenance has not been independently verified.

## Font

The 5×7 stencil bitmap alphabet is drawn in `tools/make_art.mjs` and exported as `public/assets/stencil.png` and `stencil.xml`. No external font dependency is used.

## Music

All four tracks are by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

- "Dark Fog" Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com): title screen, chests and results screen (`public/audio/dark_fog.m4a`)
- "Cyborg Ninja" Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com): fights 1 and 2 (`public/audio/fight_1.m4a`)
- "Volatile Reaction" Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com): fights 3 and 4 (`public/audio/fight_2.m4a`)
- "Killing Time" Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com): the final fight (`public/audio/fight_3.m4a`)

Downloaded from incompetech.com on 2026-11-14. Each was cut to the loudest two minutes (100 seconds for Dark Fog), crossfaded end into start so it loops, and re-encoded to AAC for the web. Nothing else was changed. The credit wording is the one his site asks for, and it is also shown on the in-game results screen.

The sound effects, the hum and the fallback music loop are generated by `tools/make_audio.mjs`.
